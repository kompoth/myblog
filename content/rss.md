Wow, I am flattered that you got to this page!

If you want to be kept posted about updates in this blog (I usually post once in forever so it shouldn't bother you),
you can subscribe to my RSS feed. In short, it is just a machine-readable version of the [posts](/posts) section. You
need to install any RSS client (I prefer Feeder for Android) and configure it to read this URL:

```plain
https://me.kmicic.net/posts/index.xml
```

After you do that, your RSS client will poll my blog from time to time and check for updates. Depending on the client it
might even send you push-notifications.

Isn't it a [~nerdy~](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HAPycrBfZ0) cool way to keep in touch?!
